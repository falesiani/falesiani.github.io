Our paper **Adaptive Width Neural Networks** has been accepted for poster presentation at the [International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR) 2026](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026), to be held at the Riocentro Convention and Event Center, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, **April 23–27, 2026**. We introduce a method to learn the width of neural network layers during training (without upper bounds) and show easy truncation and dynamic compression. Co-authors: Federico Errica, Henrik Christiansen, Viktor Zaverkin, Mathias Niepert.
