Presented our work on guidance of continuous, discrete and SO(3) diffusion models for antibody design at the [Workshop on Principles of Generative Modeling (PriGM)](https://sites.google.com/view/prigm-eurips-2025/home), held on December 7th, 2025 at the Bella Center, Copenhagen, as part of EurIPS 2025. Co-authors: Jonathan H. Warrell, Henrik Christiansen, Matheus Ferraz.
