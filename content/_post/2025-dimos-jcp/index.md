Our paper on **DIMOS** (Differentiable Molecular Simulator)—a PyTorch-based framework for machine learning–enhanced molecular dynamics and Monte Carlo simulations—has been accepted for publication in the *Journal of Chemical Physics* (J. Chem. Phys. **163**, 182501 (2025)). DIMOS integrates classical force fields, ML interatomic potentials, and hybrid ML/MM approaches. Co-authors: Henrik Christiansen, Takashi Maruyama, Federico Errica, Viktor Zaverkin, Makoto Takamoto. Documentation: [dimos.henrik-christiansen.net](https://dimos.henrik-christiansen.net).
